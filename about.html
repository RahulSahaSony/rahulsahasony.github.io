About Me

I'm currently pursuing a Master of Science in Business Analytics at The George Washington University, where I focus on the intersection of data science, machine learning, and business decision-making. My journey began with a Bachelor's in Business Administration with a concentration in Finance, Banking, and Insurance, which provided me with a strong foundation in business principles and financial analysis.

My professional experience spans multiple domains, from founding and leading growth at a tech startup (Adferry) to managing operations at Wonderlo Inc., where I achieved a 4x increase in company revenue through effective inventory monetization. I've also worked as a Senior Analyst at Quantanite, honing my data processing and analysis skills.

My research interests lie in interpretable and responsible machine learning, optimization and decision analytics, and human-centered AI. I'm particularly passionate about applying these techniques to solve real-world problems in business and public policy. I believe that data-driven insights, when combined with ethical considerations, can lead to better decision-making and more equitable outcomes.

Currently, I'm working as an Extern at the International Monetary Fund, where I'm applying my analytical skills to budget tracking and compliance documentation. I'm also actively involved in the tech community as a volunteer at various conferences and as a member of the American Association for the Advancement of Science.
