About Me

From Dhaka to Washington, DC, my career has been shaped by one constant: using data to build, scale, and improve real-world systems.


I bring 7+ years of experience across operations, growth strategy, and programmatic advertising. My work includes data-driven initiatives with the International Monetary Fund (IMF), operating at the intersection of data, technology, and revenue. Previously, I served as a Senior Analyst, Operations Manager, and founding team member at a programmatic ad-tech startup, where I helped scale programmatic revenue from the ground up.


I recently completed a STEM-designated MS in Business Analytics at The George Washington University, strengthening my expertise in machine learning, optimization, time series forecasting, and responsible AI. Combined with my industry background, this allows me to translate complex data into clear, actionable insights that support better decision-making.


I’m especially interested in roles where I can take ownership, learn quickly, and support teams wherever needed. I value clear processes, clean data, thoughtful feedback, and delivering results on time.


Always open to connecting with people who believe data isn’t just about dashboards—but about building better systems and better outcomes.
