Our Story

From Startup to Global Leader

Founded in 2009 with a single office and a team of 12 professionals, NexaCore was built on one conviction: that businesses deserve an outsourcing partner that truly understands their industry, their culture, and their ambitions.

Over 15 years, we've grown to 10,000+ professionals across 28 countries, serving clients ranging from high-growth startups to Fortune 500 enterprises. Yet our core philosophy remains unchanged — we succeed when our clients succeed.

Today, NexaCore is recognized as a Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for BPO Services and holds ISO 9001, ISO 27001, and SOC 2 Type II certifications, ensuring world-class quality and security for every engagement.
