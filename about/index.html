About Me

From Dhaka to Washington, DC, my career has been defined by one constant: using data to build, scale, and optimize real-world business systems.


I bring 7+ years of experience across operations, growth strategy, and programmatic advertising. My work includes data-driven initiatives with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) at the intersection of data, technology, and revenue. I have also served as Senior Analyst, Operations Manager, and Founding Team Member at a programmatic ad-tech startup, where I helped scale programmatic revenue from the ground up.


I recently completed my Master of Science in Business Analytics (STEM) from The George Washington University, where I strengthened my expertise in machine learning, optimization, time series forecasting, and responsible AI. This academic foundation, combined with my industry background, enables me to translate complex data into clear, actionable insights that drive strategic decision-making.


I’m especially interested in opportunities where I take ownership of my work and support teams wherever needed. I may not know everything on day one, but I learn fast and take feedback seriously. I value clear processes, clean data, and delivering results on time.


Always open to connecting with professionals who believe data isn’t just about dashboards but about building better systems and better outcomes.
